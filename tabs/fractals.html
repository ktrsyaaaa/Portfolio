Fractals

2024

Interactive Installation

Interactive installation that involves a projected digital simulation that responds to viewers presence and sound. The visuals are made of 3D scans of my university campus, turned into particles. Through motion and rotation these scans create abstract and geometric shapes.

The system reacts to viewer’s proximity and audio input. Six interconnected parameters control movement, speed, rotation, and force. A change in any single parameter triggers a chain reaction that affects all others and determines the visuals.

The work explores how small, often unnoticed actions can influence the system’s behaviour.

Software: Unity, Blender

Imputs: Kinect, Microphone
