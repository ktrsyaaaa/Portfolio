Alternative Archive

2026

Web Archive

Alternative Archive is my experimental approach to digital archives.

Archives shape how we remember the past. When documenting historical objects, text and events the archivist selects material that is important to preserve. Depending on the viewpoint of the entity that is creating the archive, some historical information gets highlighted, while others gets distorted or even forgotten.

In this project I am creating a digital alternative archive, where I treat 3D scans as preservation material, while the system determines what data gets lost, distorted and transformed. The distortion is based on random data of the device that is accessing, for example time of accessing the webpage.

More info on the concept can be found here.

Software: Blender, Polycam, Three.js

Each scan is interactive by camera movement.
