Unconventional Archive

2026

Interactive Installation

A digital archive of 3D-scanned objects collected from the local Ukrainian community.

Due to the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2022, millions of Ukrainians were forced to leave their homes, with more than 90,000 now living in Prague. Even while living far from home, many carry with them small objects that preserve a connection to their homeland.

Software: Unity, Blender, RealityScan, Ableton

Setup: Azure Kinect, 12 Screens
